# Activity 5 — Copilot in PowerPoint

> **Synthetic data only:** All names, figures, events, and operational details in this activity are fictional and do not represent Louis Dreyfus Company operations.

## Purpose
Turn the fictional weekly APAC operations brief and workbook insights into a concise, leadership-ready presentation.

**Suggested time:** 10–12 minutes  
**Required files:** `Synthetic Weekly Operations Brief.docx` and `Synthetic APAC Operations Data.xlsx`. A participant’s revised Word brief may be used instead.

## Before Copilot
You would collect content from multiple files, decide what belongs on each slide, create the storyline, shorten the text, and write speaker notes manually.

## With Copilot
You can create a presentation from source files, reorganize it around an executive storyline, strengthen slide messages, and generate speaker notes for review.

## Why it is useful
Copilot connects work already completed in Word and Excel. You do not need to restart the communication process when moving from analysis to presentation.

## Setup
1. Confirm `Synthetic Weekly Operations Brief.docx` and `Synthetic APAC Operations Data.xlsx` are saved in OneDrive or SharePoint and accessible to Copilot.
2. Open PowerPoint and start a new presentation with Copilot.

## Step-by-step prompts

### 1. Create the presentation
Reference the source files when prompted, then ask:

> Create a 6-slide leadership presentation using the weekly APAC operations brief and operations workbook. Cover the executive summary, key performance insights, regional or business-area highlights, risks, actions, and next steps. Use concise language and do not add facts that are not in the source files.

### 2. Improve the storyline
> Organize this presentation into a clear executive storyline: current situation, important insights, risks, recommended actions, and next steps.

### 3. Add or refine slides
> Add a slide showing the most important planned-versus-actual differences, using only figures supported by the workbook.

> Add a slide summarizing the items that need immediate leadership attention.

> Rewrite the slide titles so that each title communicates the main message of the slide.

### 4. Adjust audience and tone
> Rewrite this presentation for a senior leadership audience. Make the language concise, confident, and action-oriented without overstating certainty.

### 5. Prepare to present
> Add speaker notes for each slide for an 8-minute briefing. Keep the notes aligned with the source files.

Then ask:

> Summarize this presentation in three key takeaways and suggest a stronger presentation title.

## Participant reflection and adoption question
- What did Copilot accelerate, and where did you need to reshape the story or visual emphasis?
- Which recurring update or briefing could begin from an existing document instead of a blank presentation?

## Key takeaway
Copilot in PowerPoint helps transform existing Microsoft 365 content into a structured presentation, improve the executive storyline, and prepare the presenter with focused speaker notes.

## Verify before using the output
Trace every figure and claim to the Word brief or Excel workbook. Check slide titles, charts, labels, dates, risks, recommendations, design, and speaker notes. Remove unsupported content and confirm the final storyline reflects the intended audience and business judgment.

# Activity 1 — Copilot in Teams

> **Synthetic data only:** All names, figures, events, and operational details in this activity are fictional and do not represent Louis Dreyfus Company operations.

## Purpose
Catch up on a fictional APAC weekly operations discussion, identify what was decided, and prepare a useful follow-up without manually reviewing the entire conversation.

**Suggested time:** 10–12 minutes  
**Required material:** `Synthetic_Teams_Meeting_Transcript.md`, made available by the facilitator as a Teams meeting recap/transcript, chat, or channel discussion.

## Before Copilot
You would scroll through messages or replay parts of a meeting, copy notes elsewhere, and manually separate decisions and actions from general discussion.

## With Copilot
You can ask targeted questions, create a structured recap, identify unresolved issues, and draft a follow-up while keeping the source conversation available for checking.

## Why it is useful
Copilot is not limited to a generic summary. You can continue asking what changed, where viewpoints differed, what remains unresolved, and what you need to do next.

## Setup
1. Open the facilitator-prepared Teams meeting recap, transcript, chat, or channel based on `Synthetic_Teams_Meeting_Transcript.md`.
2. Open Copilot for the conversation.
3. Keep the source transcript visible so you can validate the answers.

## Step-by-step prompts

### 1. Catch up quickly
> Summarize this APAC operations discussion. Organize the summary under Key Updates, Decisions, Risks, and Action Items.

Then ask:

> What are the most important points I should know before responding?

### 2. Find decisions and actions
> List the decisions made and the action items. Include owners and due dates only when they were explicitly mentioned.

Then ask:

> Which questions or issues remain unresolved?

### 3. Explore the discussion
> What changed during the discussion, and were different viewpoints expressed? Base the answer only on the transcript.

### 4. Prepare a follow-up
> Draft a concise follow-up message for the APAC operations team. Confirm the decisions, list the next actions, and politely highlight unresolved items. Do not invent owners, dates, or commitments.

Review the draft; do not post it unless the facilitator instructs you to do so.

### Optional live-meeting prompts
> What did I miss?

> What viewpoints have been shared about the current issue?

> Suggest two questions I can ask to help the group reach a decision.

## Participant reflection and adoption question
- What manual steps did Copilot reduce, and what still required your judgment?
- Which recurring meeting, channel, or group chat would you most like to catch up on faster?

## Key takeaway
Copilot in Teams helps people catch up faster, identify decisions and actions, and participate more effectively without manually reviewing an entire conversation or transcript.

## Verify before using the output
Check every name, figure, date, decision, owner, and due date against the transcript. Confirm that unresolved issues have not been presented as decisions. Copilot provides a starting point—not automatic approval.

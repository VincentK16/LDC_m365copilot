# Microsoft 365 Copilot Hands-On Experience

A practical learning experience designed to help participants discover how Microsoft 365 Copilot can reduce repetitive work, improve the quality of everyday outputs, and create more time for judgment, collaboration, and decision-making.

> **Copilot does not replace your expertise. It helps you spend less time searching, summarizing, formatting, and starting from scratch, so you can spend more time thinking, deciding, and improving.**

## Why This Session Matters

Modern work is often spread across meetings, chats, email threads, documents, spreadsheets, and presentations. Important information can be difficult to find, understand, and turn into action.

Microsoft 365 Copilot works within the apps people already use:

| App | Before Copilot | With Copilot |
|---|---|---|
| Teams | Review long conversations or meeting transcripts manually | Catch up, identify decisions and actions, and explore unresolved questions |
| Outlook | Read every reply and write responses from a blank page | Summarize threads, clarify requests, draft responses, and improve tone |
| Word | Organize rough notes and repeatedly rewrite content | Create a structured first draft, identify gaps, and adapt writing for an audience |
| Excel | Inspect tables, build formulas, and search for exceptions manually | Ask questions in natural language, find patterns, create formulas, and communicate insights |
| PowerPoint | Gather content and design a storyline slide by slide | Build from existing files, improve the narrative, and prepare speaker notes |

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of the session, participants will be able to:

- Use Copilot to catch up on Teams conversations and meetings
- Understand lengthy Outlook email threads and prepare clearer replies
- Transform working notes into a structured business brief in Word
- Analyze operational data and identify exceptions in Excel
- Turn Word and Excel content into a leadership-ready PowerPoint presentation
- Write clearer prompts and critically review Copilot output
- Identify one repeatable task where Copilot can provide immediate value

Save the files in OneDrive or SharePoint and confirm that participants can access them before the session.

Teams and Outlook activities use each participant's own approved work content. These exercises should be completed privately, and participants are not required to share their source information or Copilot results.

## A Simple Way to Experience the Value

Use this five-step approach during every activity:

1. **Before:** Consider how you would normally complete the task and where the effort goes.
2. **Try:** Use Copilot with a clear prompt and relevant source content.
3. **Compare:** Notice what became faster, clearer, or easier to discover.
4. **Verify:** Check the facts, figures, tone, assumptions, owners, dates, and recommendations.
5. **Commit:** Choose one real task where you will use the technique after the session.

## Prompting Tips

A useful prompt normally includes:

- **Task:** What do you want Copilot to do?
- **Context:** What information should it use?
- **Audience:** Who will read or receive the result?
- **Output:** What structure, length, or format do you need?
- **Boundaries:** What should Copilot avoid assuming or inventing?

Example:

> Using only the content in this document, draft a one-page weekly operations brief for senior leadership. Organize it under Executive Summary, Key Updates, Risks and Issues, Decisions, and Next Actions. Do not invent missing owners, dates, figures, or commitments.

## Responsible Use

- Review Copilot output before using or sharing it.
- Verify important claims against the source content.
- Do not treat uncertain information as a confirmed fact.
- Use only content appropriate for the organization's approved Microsoft 365 environment.
- Avoid displaying confidential, personal, HR, legal, medical, or otherwise sensitive information during group training.
- The supplied Word and Excel materials are fictional training assets and do not represent actual Louis Dreyfus Company operations.

## An Inspiring Message

Copilot is most valuable when it becomes part of the way we work, not simply a feature we try once.

Start with one task that regularly consumes your time: catching up on a meeting, understanding an email thread, structuring a document, investigating a workbook, or preparing a presentation. Let Copilot create the starting point, then apply your experience to question it, refine it, and make the final decision.

The opportunity is not to do the same work faster and fill the saved time with more administration. The opportunity is to redirect our attention toward customers, colleagues, creativity, problem-solving, and better decisions.

> **Let Copilot handle more of the first draft, so people can bring more of their best thinking to the final result.**

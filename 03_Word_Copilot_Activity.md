# Activity 3 — Copilot in Word

> **Synthetic data only:** All names, figures, events, and operational details in this activity are fictional and do not represent Louis Dreyfus Company operations.

## Purpose
Transform rough weekly APAC operations notes into a concise, structured one-page brief.

**Suggested time:** 10–12 minutes  
**Required files:** `Synthetic Weekly Operations Notes.docx` as the Word working file and `Synthetic_Weekly_Operations_Notes.md` as the source/reference copy.

## Before Copilot
You would review the notes, decide on headings, move information into the right sections, rewrite unclear sentences, and check whether important information is missing.

## With Copilot
You can summarize the source, identify gaps, generate a structured first draft, rewrite it for a specific audience, and convert actions into a usable table.

## Why it is useful
Copilot helps overcome the blank page. You remain the editor and decision-maker, but begin with a structured draft rather than an empty document.

## Setup
1. Open `Synthetic Weekly Operations Notes.docx` in Word from OneDrive or SharePoint.
2. Keep `Synthetic_Weekly_Operations_Notes.md` available as a reference copy if provided.
3. Open Copilot in Word.

## Step-by-step prompts

### 1. Understand the content
> Summarize this document. Identify the main APAC operations updates, business impact, risks, decisions, and required actions.

Then ask:

> What important information is unclear or missing from these notes? Do not fill the gaps with assumptions.

### 2. Create the brief
> Using only the content in this document, draft a one-page weekly APAC operations brief. Use the headings Executive Summary, Key Updates, Risks and Issues, Decisions, and Next Actions. Do not invent facts that are not in the source content.

Insert the draft into the document.

### 3. Improve the writing
Select the draft and ask:

> Rewrite this for a senior business audience. Make it concise, clear, and action-oriented while preserving the source meaning.

Then ask:

> Convert the Next Actions section into a table with Action, Owner, Due Date, and Status. Leave fields blank when the source does not provide the information.

### 4. Review the result
> Review this brief for clarity and consistency. Suggest a stronger title and three key takeaways. Flag any statement that may not be supported by the source notes.

## Participant reflection and adoption question
- How much effort moved from structuring and rewriting to reviewing and deciding?
- What document do you repeatedly create from similar notes, emails, or previous reports?

## Key takeaway
Copilot in Word helps turn unstructured information into a polished business document while improving organization, tone, and readability.

## Verify before using the output
Compare the brief with the original notes. Check names, figures, dates, risks, decisions, actions, owners, and status. Ensure blank or uncertain fields remain clearly identified and that Copilot has not invented business impact or recommendations.

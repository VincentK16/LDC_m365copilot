# Activity 2 — Copilot in Outlook

> **Synthetic data only:** All names, figures, events, and operational details in this activity are fictional and do not represent Louis Dreyfus Company operations.

## Purpose
Understand a fictional APAC operations email thread and prepare a clear, audience-appropriate response.

**Suggested time:** 10–12 minutes  
**Required material:** `Synthetic_Outlook_Email_Thread.md`, prepared by the facilitator as an Outlook training thread.

## Before Copilot
You would read every reply, reconstruct who is asking for what, identify the latest position, and compose a response from a blank page.

## With Copilot
You can summarize the thread, identify the request and missing information, generate a response, adjust it for the audience, and use coaching to improve clarity and tone.

## Why it is useful
Copilot combines understanding and communication in one workflow. You spend less effort reconstructing the thread and more effort making the right decision and checking the final response.

## Setup
1. Open the Outlook training thread based on `Synthetic_Outlook_Email_Thread.md`.
2. Confirm that it is the synthetic APAC operations scenario.
3. Plan to save a draft only. Do not send the training message unless the facilitator explicitly instructs you to do so.

## Step-by-step prompts

### 1. Summarize the thread
Use **Summary by Copilot**, or ask:

> Summarize this APAC operations email thread in five bullets. Highlight the request, current status, concerns, decisions, and next actions.

Then ask:

> What information is missing before a final decision can be made?

### 2. Draft a response
Select **Reply**, open Copilot, and enter:

> Draft a professional reply that acknowledges the issue, confirms only the agreed actions, asks for the missing information, and proposes a clear next step. Do not invent facts, dates, owners, or commitments.

### 3. Adjust the message
Try one or more refinements:

> Make the message shorter and more direct.

> Rewrite this for a senior leadership audience.

> Make the tone collaborative and confident.

### 4. Use Coaching by Copilot
Use **Coaching by Copilot** to review tone, clarity, and reader impact. Apply only the suggestions that make the message more accurate and useful.

## Participant reflection and adoption question
- Did Copilot make the request and next action clearer? What still required human judgment?
- Which type of email takes the most time for you to understand or respond to?

## Key takeaway
Copilot in Outlook reduces the time needed to understand lengthy threads and helps create clear, audience-appropriate responses while keeping the user in control of the final message.

## Verify before using the output
Check recipients, names, figures, dates, decisions, commitments, requested information, and tone against the complete thread. Remove unsupported claims and confirm the draft is suitable for its audience. Do not send the synthetic training email unless instructed.

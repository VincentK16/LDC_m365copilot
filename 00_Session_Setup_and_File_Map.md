# Louis Dreyfus Company — Microsoft 365 Copilot Session Setup and File Map

## Session goal
Experience how Microsoft 365 Copilot can turn a connected APAC operations scenario—from a meeting and email thread through notes, data analysis, and a leadership presentation—into clear actions and decision-ready content.

> **Fictional training data only:** All names, figures, events, locations, dates, and operational details in the session materials are fictional training data. They do not represent Louis Dreyfus Company operations. Use only the supplied synthetic materials or other approved, non-confidential content.

## Recommended run order

| Order | Activity | Suggested time | Outcome |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Teams Copilot | 10–12 min | Catch up on the APAC operations discussion and extract decisions, risks, and actions. |
| 2 | Outlook Copilot | 10–12 min | Understand the follow-up thread and prepare a clear response. |
| 3 | Word Copilot | 10–12 min | Turn weekly notes into a structured one-page operations brief. |
| 4 | Excel Copilot | 10–12 min | Analyze APAC operations data for variances, trends, and exceptions. |
| 5 | PowerPoint Copilot | 10–12 min | Convert the brief and workbook insights into a leadership presentation. |

Allow an additional 5–10 minutes for setup and final reflection.

## Connected synthetic scenario
Participants join a fictional weekly APAC operations review. They catch up on the meeting, respond to an email thread, structure the weekly notes, analyze operational data, and prepare a concise leadership briefing. Each activity carries forward the context of the previous one.

## Facilitator preparation
1. Confirm participant access to Microsoft 365 Copilot in Teams, Outlook, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
2. Place the Word brief/notes and Excel workbook in OneDrive or SharePoint, with permissions tested in advance.
3. Prepare a Teams meeting recap, transcript, chat, or channel using `Synthetic_Teams_Meeting_Transcript.md`.
4. Prepare an Outlook training thread using `Synthetic_Outlook_Email_Thread.md`. Tell participants whether to save a draft only; do not send training messages unless explicitly instructed.
5. Confirm the Excel data is formatted as a table and that Copilot can access it.
6. Keep the Markdown and JSON files available as facilitator reference copies for checking generated outputs.
7. Share the six participant guides and explain the recommended run order.
8. Remind participants that Copilot output is a starting point, not automatic approval.

## Adoption method for every activity
1. **Before:** Identify how the task is normally completed and where effort is spent.
2. **Try:** Run the prompts and review the result.
3. **Compare:** Notice what Copilot accelerated, improved, or surfaced.
4. **Verify:** Check facts, figures, sources, tone, assumptions, owners, and dates.
5. **Commit:** Name one repeatable task where the technique could be used next week.

> **Core message:** Copilot shifts effort from searching, summarizing, formatting, and starting from scratch to reviewing, deciding, and improving.
